Escalation Practice Coach

An upset customer is on the line. Reply out loud or type it, then see how you did on five de-escalation skills.

Nothing you say or type is saved. Voice uses your browser’s speech-to-text and works best in Chrome or Edge.

D
Dana, customer

Scoring reads your words only, so it can’t judge tone of voice yet.

How it’s scored

Five skills, two points each

SkillWhat earns full marks
Acknowledges the emotionNames what the customer is feeling, in your own words
Takes ownershipNo blaming the customer, the system, or another team
Plain languageNo jargon or policy-speak
Clear next stepSays what happens next and when
Stays in controlCalm boundaries, especially when they ask for a manager

Before AI scoring goes live, I score a set of sample replies myself and tune the rubric until the AI agrees with me. A person also spot-checks scored replies over time.
