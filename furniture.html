The striped chest

This cherry chest came in with a chipped, water-marked top and worn edges. I repaired and primed it, hand-painted navy and pale blue stripes across the top and base, added scalloped trim down the sides, and lined the drawers in gingham.

How I work on a piece

The same habits as my day job

Every piece starts with a diagnosis and ends with a finish I'd be happy to live with. In between, it's a lot of testing small before committing big.

Assess

Check the structure, the wood, and the existing finish to decide what's worth saving and what needs fixing first.

Strip and repair

Remove the old finish, fix joints and veneer, fill damage, and sand back to a clean surface.

Prime and block

Choose a primer for the wood in front of me. When tannins bled through the paint on one dresser, I switched from a shellac-based primer to an oil-based stain blocker.

Finish and style

Paint, stain, or seal for durability, update the hardware, and photograph the result.
