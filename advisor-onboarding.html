Onboarding 2,000+ advisors into Spotify Customer Service

An end-to-end onboarding redesign for a global support team, including a short animated video series that shows new advisors the world they're joining.

The goal

Get new advisors confident, faster

Spotify's support advisors handle customers, creators, and partners across many languages and time zones, around the clock. As the primary learning partner for that population, I redesigned onboarding end to end, from the first days of training through nesting on live contacts, so new advisors could reach proficiency sooner without losing quality.

The video series

Welcome to Customer Service, in eight short films

Before system training starts, new advisors need context: who Spotify serves, how support is organized, and why their work matters. An eight-part animated series covers that ground in one to two minutes per film, with captions for a global audience.

Design choices

Why short films

Context before systemsAdvisors learn tools faster when they already understand the organization the tools serve.
One idea per filmOne to two minutes each, so every film can be watched, rewatched, or updated on its own.
Captioned for a global teamOn-screen captions support advisors working in a second language.
Reusable beyond day oneThe same films work for refreshers and for cross-functional partners learning how support works.
Results

A third faster to proficiency

  • Time to proficiency fell from 60 days to 40 (33% faster ramp)
  • Onboarding shortened from five days to three (40%)
  • Nesting shortened from four weeks to two (50%)
