Pinned vs. Owned

I had 170 outfit ideas saved on Pinterest and every piece of clothing I own catalogued in Alta, but no idea how much of that board I could already wear. So I built a tool with Claude that breaks every pin into pieces and checks each one against my closet.

The question

Is my Pinterest board a shopping list, or is it already in my closet?

Saving outfits is easy. Working out which ones I can actually put together, and which single purchase would open up the most new outfits, is the hard part, and neither app does it. Alta knows my clothes. Pinterest knows my taste. Nothing connects them.

How it works

From two apps to one answer

Pull the pins

Using Claude in Chrome, Claude read every pin on my public Outfits board: 171 images.

Pull the closet

Alta has no export button, but its web app does. Claude read my closet there while I was signed in: 154 items, each with a category, colors and fabric.

Break each pin into pieces

Claude looked at each image and listed the top, layer, bottoms and shoes. Bags, jewelry and sunglasses were left out.

Match each piece three ways

Own it (I have this), close enough (I have something that does the same job), or don't own.

Score and rank

Each outfit becomes "wear it now", "one piece away" or "two or more away". For every missing piece, the tool counts how many outfits it would complete.

Shoes turned out to be the biggest gap. That says more about my catalog than my closet, though: I'd only added 5 pairs to Alta. The quickest win was adding the rest of my shoes to Alta, not buying any.

Why it's here

It's a skills gap analysis, for clothes

This is the same move I make at work. Define the target (the outfits I want to wear), take stock of what's already there (the closet), and find the smallest set of gaps that unlocks the most. A pair of black jeans is the closet version of the one module that gets most of a team to proficient.

It's also a good example of how I like to work with AI. Claude did the slow parts: pulling data out of two apps, looking at 170 images and keeping the matching consistent. I made the judgment calls about what counts as "close enough", then checked the matches by eye.

Pin images belong to the people who made them, so this page only shows my side of the match. The full lookbook, with pins, stays private.
