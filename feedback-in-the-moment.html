Feedback in the Moment

A scenario-based learning experience that helps Netflix employees build everyday feedback habits, one moment at a time.

Learning experience designer
Design exercise
1 week
Docebo LMS
React, Claude
The challenge

Feedback was only happening during formal reviews

Netflix's Learning and People Development team saw employees engaging with feedback only during formal 360 review cycles. Outside those windows, the everyday feedback that changes behavior wasn't happening.

Their existing resources weren't fixing it. The Feedback Workshop was one-size-fits-all, and the Feedback Toolkit was thorough but overwhelming. Neither reached people during their actual work.

The brief

Design one 5 to 7 minute digital learning experience, mobile-friendly and hosted in Docebo, that helps employees build feedback habits across four regions with different cultural norms.

Research and insight

People knew how. They weren't doing it.

Netflix shared sentiment data from all four regions. Use of feedback tools dropped sharply outside 360 cycles, and employees said they wanted to give feedback more than they did. The reasons varied by person as much as by region. A new hire in London and a director in São Paulo might hold back for different reasons, or for the same one.

Before designing anything, I mapped what a learning experience could realistically change.

What learning can change

  • A simple, repeatable approach for in-the-moment feedback
  • Practice reps in realistic, culturally nuanced contexts
  • Adapting style across relationships, power dynamics, and cultural norms

What needs the wider business

  • Feedback infrastructure that lasts beyond 360 cycles
  • Managers modeling the behavior by asking for feedback as well as giving it
  • Feedback woven into rituals like 1:1s, retros, and milestones

A five-minute experience won't change a culture on its own. It can still be the strongest lever L&D owns, as long as the business handles the rest.

Design approach

A four-week system

I designed four weeks of spaced learning, delivered through Slack into Docebo. Each week, a notification takes the learner into one 5 to 7 minute scenario.

Week 1 · prototyped

Give it

Initiating feedback in a real moment, even when it feels uncomfortable.

Week 2

Receive it

Hearing hard feedback without shutting down or getting defensive.

Week 3

Seek it

Actively asking for the feedback you're not getting.

Week 4

Do it

Having a real conversation, with support on hand.

Spaced outOne session doesn't build a habit. Weekly pacing leaves time to practice between modules.
Built on scenariosEvery module starts from a familiar workplace moment, and learners make the choices themselves.
Adapted to each person's barrierA check-in asks learners what holds them back, then sends them down the matching path.
Ends in real actionEvery module ends with a commitment. It's done when they've had the conversation.

Key design decisions

Four choices that shaped the experience

Netflix IP over generic scenarios

An emotional hook only Netflix has

Clips from Emily in Paris make it feel like Netflix rather than training.

Slack over LMS notifications

Meet people where they work

Practice feels like part of the day, and Docebo's Slack integration makes it easy to run.

Weekly pacing over self-paced

Time to have the real conversation

One module a week leaves room to practice before the next skill drops.

Self-selection over assigned paths

Route by need

Two very different employees who name the same barrier get the same path.

What I'd do next

This prototype is module 1 of 4

Complete the system

Build Receive it, Seek it, and Do it to the same depth, each with its own scenarios and clips.

Partner for sustainment

After week 4, shift to micro-prompts in standups, 1:1s, and retros, in partnership with People Management and Operations.

Build the measurement layer

Measure whether feedback conversations happen more often and whether barriers shift, using Docebo xAPI data alongside sentiment data.

Pilot, then scale

Run one region first and use real learner data to tune scenarios, pacing, and routing before going global.
