Luminary and its AI Assistant are fictional. The scenario is invented; the method is the one I've used on real launches.

Why I built it

Showing enablement design I can't share from real launches

Most of my launch work can't be shared publicly, so I built this to show how I approach enablement in practice: a kit for a fictional AI feature launch that takes three very different audiences to launch-ready through role-specific content, realistic scenarios, and a knowledge check.

Approach

One launch, three tailored experiences

Role-based learning pathsEngineers need architecture and debugging context, PMs need positioning and timelines, support needs FAQs and escalation paths. I used Action Mapping to find the decisions each audience makes after launch, and designed backwards.
Scenario-based practiceReading about a product and handling a live situation are different things. Scenarios put learners in realistic, ambiguous situations where they have to apply what they learned.
Embedded assessmentThe knowledge check is a readiness gate (Kirkpatrick level 2). In production, its data would show which topics need reinforcement.
Connected to real workIt mirrors how I ran launch readiness for Audiobooks and Wrapped at Spotify: role-specific content, hands-on practice, and feedback loops.

How it would launch

Part of a launch campaign, not a standalone course

The kit would live as a web app connected to the LMS for tracking. The core module (overview, scenarios, and knowledge check) is required for all three audiences before launch day; role deep dives are recommended. It would ship alongside product documentation, internal comms, and team briefings.

Measuring impact

Designed with all four Kirkpatrick levels in mind

LevelWhat I'd measure
1. ReactionA short survey on relevance, with prompts on what was missing or unclear
2. LearningKnowledge check and scenario scores together; scenarios are the stronger signal of applied understanding
3. BehaviorTicket quality, escalation patterns, and manager observations after launch
4. ResultsFewer AI-related tickets, a lower escalation rate, and higher feature adoption, correlated with completion data
