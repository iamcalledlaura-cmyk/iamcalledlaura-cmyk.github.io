Launch-ready before launch day

Every year, about 1,000 new advisors joined just before Spotify Wrapped. They needed to handle launch-day contacts before a single one existed, so I built a simulated environment where they could practice them.

The problem

You can’t learn launch day on the job, because the job doesn’t exist yet

Wrapped questions arrive all at once, on launch day. Before that there are no real contacts to shadow, take, or learn from, yet a whole new cohort has to be ready to handle the spike from the first hour.

  • No live contact volume to practice on before launch
  • A large, brand-new cohort every season
  • Hiring about a month ahead to buy ramp time, which was slow and costly
  • No room to dip on CSAT or SLA during the busiest moment of the year
The approach

Recreate launch day before launch day

Training blended in-person sessions with Rise courses. Inside Rise, I coded simulations into code blocks, so advisors could work full Wrapped contacts end to end long before real ones arrived.

Gather requirements with SMEs

Worked with subject matter experts to define what advisors needed to know and do for each launch.

Analyze past launches

Analyzed data from previous Wrapped seasons to find what drove contacts at launch, and built the practice around it.

Listen to past advisors

Ran empathy interviews with advisors from earlier seasons about where launch day felt hard, and designed for those moments.

Build the environment

Simulations coded into Rise, so advisors could work whole contacts on their own, as many times as they needed, alongside in-person sessions.

With realistic practice built on that research, cohorts could start two weeks before launch instead of a month.

      Impact

      Half the lead time, and customers didn’t feel the surge

      • Hire-to-launch lead time cut from about a month to two weeks
      • About 1,000 new advisors ramped each season, across four Wrapped launches (2022–2025)
      • CSAT and SLA held steady despite a huge increase in contact volume at launch
      • Two fewer weeks of pre-launch ramp for every seasonal hire

      The best measure was the one customers saw: launch day looked like any other day in the queue.

      Reflection

      What transfers to any seasonal surge

      Practice what doesn’t exist yetIf real contacts can’t teach it, simulate them.
      Start from evidenceSME requirements, past contact data, and advisors’ own stories, before anything was built.
      Blend the room and the screenIn person for context, simulations for reps.
      Measure on the queueCSAT and SLA under peak volume, not course completions.
